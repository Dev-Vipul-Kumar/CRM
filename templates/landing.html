Features

Everything in One Platform

From the moment a contract is drafted to the day it's signed, every step lives inside ContractFlow.

Contract Lifecycle Management
Create and track contracts with auto-generated unique numbers (CTR-YYYY-XXXX). Manage vendor details, dates, values and all metadata through a controlled status pipeline — from Draft right through to Active, Renewal or Archive.
Draft → Review → Approval → Signature → Active
Version Control
Every document upload creates a new version. Full history is preserved — previous revisions are never deleted. Download any version at any time.
Approval Workflow
Structured review and approval pipeline — submit, review, approve, request changes or reject. Full comment and timestamp history preserved on every decision.
Digital Signatures
Canvas-based digital signature pad. Request signatures from specific users, capture drawn signatures, and record the signer's IP address and exact timestamp for each contract.
Signature captured & stored

Renewal Alerts
Automated expiry monitoring with configurable thresholds — 30-day warning, 15-day reminder, 7-day urgent, and critical alerts so no contract lapses unnoticed.
360° Audit Trail
Every action — login, create, edit, approve, sign, download — is recorded with the acting user, timestamp and IP address. Full contract activity timeline available.
Role-Based Dashboards
Separate dashboards for Admin, Manager and Employee — each populated with live database statistics specific to that role. No hard-coded numbers.
Reports & CSV Export
Summary reports by status, department and category. Filter by date range or contract type. Export any filtered dataset to CSV instantly for offline analysis.

Digital Workflow
Workflow

From Draft to Active
in Five Steps

Every stage is tracked. Every transition is controlled by business rules.

1

Create Contract

Draft a contract with all details — vendor, dates, value — and upload the document as Version 1.

2

Submit for Approval

A manager submits the contract for review. Authorised reviewers receive an instant in-app notification.

3

Review & Approve

The reviewer approves, rejects or requests changes. Full comment and timestamp are recorded on every decision.

4

Digital Signature

Signature requests are sent to designated signers. The canvas pad captures and securely stores each signature.

5

Active & Auto-Monitored

The contract goes live. Renewal reminders fire automatically at 30, 15 and 7 days before expiry.
